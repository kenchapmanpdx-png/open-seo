How we work

We fix roofs. In 2019 Maria Lopez opened our first shop on Elm Street in Portland with two used trucks and a loan from her uncle that she paid back in four years. Rain is hard. Last March our crew of 6 replaced a cedar roof on a 1920 house in Salem, and the owner, Tom Reyes, sent us a photo of the dry attic after the first storm.

Call us first. We buy shingles from Pacific Supply in Tigard because they stock the 30 year line we trust and they will swap a bad box the same day we call them. Crews start early. Most jobs in Lake Oswego take 2 or 3 days, but a steep roof with old tar paper can take a full week when we find rot under the top layer.

What it costs

Bids are free. Our lead roofer, Ben Cho, has done this work for 18 years and he checks every flashing seam himself before we pack up and haul the scrap away. Ask for Sam. In 2023 we fixed 412 roofs across Clackamas County and only 3 owners called us back for a leak, which we patched for free within a day or two.
