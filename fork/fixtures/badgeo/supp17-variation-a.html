Emergency drain cleaning in Portland

What every drain call includes

Our drain technicians answer the phone day and night and arrive with a fully stocked van.

Every visit starts with a camera inspection so you can see the blockage on a screen before any work begins.

We clear kitchen sinks, bathroom basins, floor drains and main sewer lines with a cable machine or water jetting.

Roots in clay pipes are cut back and the line is filmed again to confirm a clean run to the street.

  • Grease build-up is flushed with hot water at high pressure rather than with corrosive chemicals.
  • The technician explains the cause of the blockage and how to keep the line clear in future.
  • Work is guaranteed for ninety days, and a return visit within that time costs nothing.
  • Floors are covered with drop sheets and the work area is cleaned before the van leaves.
