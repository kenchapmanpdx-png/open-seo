Hidden FAQ

When does packet pickup open?

Packet pickup opens at the park on race morning.

Most first-time entrants ask about pace limits. There is no cut-off on the shorter distances; walkers are welcome and the sweep bike stays behind the final person. Longer rides close the course after five hours, and anyone still out is offered a lift back in the support van.
