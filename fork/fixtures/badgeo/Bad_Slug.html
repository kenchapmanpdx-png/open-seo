Bad Slug

Uppercase letters and an underscore in the slug.

Course maps are drawn by our route captain, Dana Okafor, who rides each loop twice in the week before race day. She notes gravel patches, sharp bends and narrow bridges, then adds kilometre markers. Printed copies sit at registration, and a downloadable GPX file is attached to the confirmation email.
