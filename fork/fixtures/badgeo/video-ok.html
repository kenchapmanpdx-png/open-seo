Finish line walkthrough

Transcript

From the last bridge, keep left and follow the cones along the lake. The path narrows for fifty metres beside the rowing club, then opens onto the grass straight. The finish arch is blue and white, and the clock above it shows gun time. After the line, walk on to the medal table and the water station before stopping, so the runners behind you have room to finish.
