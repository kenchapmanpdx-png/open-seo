Brake adjustment for road bikes

Rim brakes squeal when the pads are glazed, so the mechanic sands them and toes them in slightly.

Hydraulic discs feel spongy after a winter in the shed because air has crept into the hose.

A bleed kit pushes fresh mineral oil through the caliper until no bubbles reach the lever.

Before you drop the bike off

  1. Bring the bike in clean so the mechanic can see worn parts at a glance.
  2. Most repairs are finished the same afternoon if the parts are in stock.

Rotors thinner than one and a half millimetres are replaced rather than trued.

Cable brakes get new inner wires whenever the old ones show a single broken strand.

Lever reach is set for small hands on request without charging for the extra time.
