Race day checklist

Most first-timers forget at least one thing on the morning, so the welcome team keeps this short checklist pinned by the start funnel. Run through it before you leave home.

• Print your barcode or load it in the app
• Pack a water bottle and a light layer
• Pin your bib on the front, not the back
• Arrive fifteen minutes before the start

The bag drop by the cafe is unattended, so leave valuables at home.
