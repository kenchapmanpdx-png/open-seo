Gear tuning for touring bikes

Indexing drifts when a gear cable stretches during its first month of riding.

The rear derailleur hanger is checked with an alignment gauge before any barrel adjuster is turned.

A chain with more than half a percent of wear is swapped before it chews through the cassette.

Before you drop the bike off

  1. Bring the bike in clean so the mechanic can see worn parts at a glance.
  2. Most repairs are finished the same afternoon if the parts are in stock.

Friction shifters on vintage frames are cleaned and lightly greased at the pivot.

Front derailleurs are set a couple of millimetres above the big ring teeth.

Long tours with luggage need a lower gear than most riders expect on the first climb.
