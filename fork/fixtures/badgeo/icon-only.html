Share the course map

The course map for the autumn trail series is ready to share: print it for the club notice board or send it to a friend before race day.

Medals are cast by a foundry in Milwaukie using recycled brass from old plumbing fittings. Each year the design changes, featuring a local landmark such as the Saint Johns bridge or Mount Tabor reservoir. Finishers collect theirs after crossing the line, together with a reusable cup for refills.
