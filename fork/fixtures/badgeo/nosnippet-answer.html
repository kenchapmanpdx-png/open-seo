Bag drop

The bag drop sits behind the start line in the blue gazebo and opens forty minutes before the gun. Label every bag with your race number; unlabelled bags are not accepted.

Bags are handed back on the same table from twenty minutes after the first finisher. Valuables stay at your own risk, so leave keys and phones with a friend or in the car where you can.

Large rucksacks, bikes and buggies cannot be stored. If it rains the gazebo walls go down, and the marshals move the queue under the trees by the boathouse so nobody waits in the open.
