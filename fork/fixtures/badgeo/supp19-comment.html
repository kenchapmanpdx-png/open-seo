Sail loft repairs and covers

The loft repairs torn mainsails, jibs and spinnakers on a long cutting floor.

Worn stitching on the leech is restitched with ultraviolet-stable thread.

Batten pockets are rebuilt when the old webbing has frayed through.

Sail covers are cut to measure from the boom and the mast height.

Each repair gets a tag with the boat name and the date it was collected.

Winter storage keeps sails dry and folded on wide racks.

Collection and delivery runs along the marina pontoons on Fridays.
