Course guide

Our history on the lake

The club first marked out the lake loop in 2009 with chalk arrows and a borrowed tape measure. Back then the path was gravel all the way round and the boathouse still rented rowing boats on Sunday afternoons, so the start sat on the far side by the bandstand.

Over the years the council resurfaced the north bank, the bandstand was rebuilt and the start moved twice. After all of that, the course today is two laps of the lake, five kilometres in total, finishing by the cafe.

Surfaces and conditions

Winter storms bring down branches on the south bank most years, and the park rangers clear them within a day or two. Puddles gather under the willows after rain. In the end, road shoes are fine all year: the whole loop is tarmac except a short stretch of compacted gravel.
