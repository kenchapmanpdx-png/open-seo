The beginner clinic meets on Tuesday evenings at Laurelhurst Park. Coaches cover warm-ups, steady pacing and how to handle the first hill without burning out. Sessions last forty-five minutes, cost nothing for registered entrants, and finish with stretching on the grass beside the duck pond near the east gate.
