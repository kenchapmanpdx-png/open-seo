Course flyover

A drone flew the full loop the week before race day. The recording shows each turn, the short gravel stretch past the boathouse and the long straight that leads into the finish arch.

Spectators usually gather on the footbridge, where runners pass twice. Parking fills early near the main gate, so the shuttle from the library car park is the easier choice for families with strollers.
