Club kit

The club vest is navy with a single white chevron, cut from recycled polyester that dries quickly on wet winter mornings. Members order through the shop in the clubhouse every second Thursday; orders arrive within three weeks and are handed out at the Saturday parkrun.

Spare numbers, safety pins and reflective bands sit in the blue box by the door, free for anyone who forgot theirs.
