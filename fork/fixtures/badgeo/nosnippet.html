Volunteer rota

Every Saturday the run needs twelve volunteers: two timekeepers, two barcode scanners, a tail walker, a first-timers briefer and six marshals spread around the lake loop. The rota opens four weeks ahead and closes on the Wednesday before each run.

Volunteers arrive at half past eight for a short briefing by the cafe. Hi-vis vests, stopwatches and scanners are handed out from the green crate and returned straight after the last finisher.
