Fade two course notes

Course notes for the autumn trail series: every race starts at the north gate of the river park, the timing mats sit beside the boat ramp, and the finish chute closes ninety minutes after the last wave leaves the start line.

The spring series runs every Saturday morning from the river park trailhead with a timed five kilometre loop, a ten kilometre course along the levee and a one mile family walk; entry costs twenty dollars for adults and ten dollars for students, packet pickup opens at seven, and the first wave starts at eight sharp. Volunteers staff two water tables on the levee and a first aid tent at the finish.
