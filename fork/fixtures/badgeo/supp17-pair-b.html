Kayak hire at South Beach

South Beach opens onto the estuary, where the tide runs fast past the sandbar.

Kayaks are handed out from the boathouse after a short safety talk on the slipway.

Every hire includes a paddle, a buoyancy aid and a waterproof bag for phones and keys.

Children under twelve paddle in a double kayak with an adult in the back seat.

The lifeguard flag on the beach shows whether the bay is open for hire that hour.

Kayaks must be back on the sand fifteen minutes before the boathouse closes.

Wetsuits are free to borrow in spring and autumn when the water is still cold.
