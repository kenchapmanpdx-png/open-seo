Parkrun FAQ

How long is the lake run?

The lake run is five kilometres: two laps of the lake path, starting by the bandstand and finishing outside the cafe on the north bank.

Do I need to register before my first lake run?

Yes, register once online before your first lake run and bring the printed barcode or app barcode, because results cannot be recorded without it.

Can I bring my dog to the lake run?

Dogs are welcome at the lake run on a short lead, one dog per runner, and dog runners start at the back of the field to keep the path clear.
