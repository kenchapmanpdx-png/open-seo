Wheel truing for city bikes

A wheel that rubs the frame on every turn usually has a few loose spokes on one side.

Spoke tension is measured with a meter so the rim pulls straight without stressing the nipples.

Dented rims from potholes can be eased out, but a cracked sidewall means a new rim.

Before you drop the bike off

  1. Bring the bike in clean so the mechanic can see worn parts at a glance.
  2. Most repairs are finished the same afternoon if the parts are in stock.

Hub bearings are repacked with grease when they feel rough under a slow turn of the axle.

Tubeless tyres get fresh sealant every few months because the liquid dries inside the casing.

Dynamo hubs are tested with a lamp before the wheel goes back on the bike.
