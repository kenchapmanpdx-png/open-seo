Volunteer sign-in

Volunteers sign in at the blue gazebo between six and six thirty, collect a radio and a hi-vis vest, and walk to their marshal point with a printed course map. Shifts end when the sweep cyclist passes.

  1. Radio channel 4 is the course channel.
  2. Radio channel 6 reaches the medical tent.
