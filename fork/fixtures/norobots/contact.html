Contact and booking

Call 503 555 0142 between 9 am and 6 pm, Tuesday to Sunday, or write to hello@riverside-fit.example and we answer within one working day. Appointments start at 9 am, 11 am, 2 pm and 4 pm.

The workshop is at 2210 SE Division Street, Portland, Oregon 97202. Street parking is free after 6 pm; the number 4 bus stops two blocks away and there is covered bike parking at the door.

If you need to move an appointment, tell us a day ahead and we will rebook you at no charge. Late cancellations are charged half the fit price.
