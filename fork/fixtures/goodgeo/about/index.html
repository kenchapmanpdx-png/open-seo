About Riverside Bike Fitting

Maya Chen opened the workshop in 2011 after eight years as a mechanic at a Portland race team. She holds a Level 2 fitting certificate and has fitted more than three thousand riders, from commuters to stage racers.

Tom Alvarez joined in 2016. He builds every wheel by hand, trues it on a Park Tool stand and re-checks spoke tension after the first hundred miles, free of charge. He also runs the Saturday maintenance class.

We keep the workshop small on purpose: two fitters, one trainer, and no more than four fits a day, so each rider gets the full appointment without being rushed.
