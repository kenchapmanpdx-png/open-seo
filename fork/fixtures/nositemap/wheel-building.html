Hand-built wheels

Every wheel is laced by hand to the rider's weight and use. A typical gravel pair uses 28 spokes front and 32 rear, brass nipples and a rim between 23 and 25 millimetres wide inside. Prices start at 640 dollars a pair including hubs.

Lead time is four weeks from the deposit. We stock common hubs and order rims on demand. After your first hundred miles, bring the wheels back and we re-check tension and true them for free.

We do not sell carbon rims from unknown brands, and we will tell you if a cheaper stock wheel suits your riding better than a custom build.
