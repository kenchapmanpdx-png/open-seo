Riverside Bike Fitting

Riverside Bike Fitting measures riders on a static trainer, records saddle height, reach and cleat angle, and writes every number on a printed card you keep. A standard fit takes ninety minutes and costs 180 dollars; a follow-up within thirty days is free.

The workshop sits at 2210 SE Division Street, two blocks from the number 4 bus stop. Bring your own shoes, pedals and riding kit. We fit road, gravel and touring bikes, and we build wheels to order with a four-week lead time.

We also build wheels by hand. Most riders book after knee or neck pain on longer rides. We cannot diagnose injuries, so we ask about pain first and refer to a physiotherapist when a change of position is not enough.
