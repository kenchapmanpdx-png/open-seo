## Class

`checks/url-variants.ts` sends the `/index.html` probe through `hopProblem` (the "one permanent hop to the target" rule for host variants). Any chain that does not end at the homepage gets "ends at …" and the "live duplicate" title, even when it ends in 404 / 410.

## Steps

1. **Red test** (public entry point: the url-variants check, `checks/url-variants.test.ts`): an `/index.html` probe with `redirectChain` [301 → `/index.html/`] and final status 404 → today a P3-03 "is a live duplicate" finding. Save to `fork/runs/b25/wo004-red.txt`.
2. **Class fix** in the `/index.html` branch: a chain whose final status is 404 or 410 is not a duplicate; P3-03 passes for that probe with the note "ends in 404". For Ken: say "index.html must reach the homepage" to revert.

## Guards

- `/index.html` answering 200 with no redirect still opens P3-03 (the five sites above).
- `/index.html` → 302 → `/` still opens the temporary-hop finding.

## Do not

- Change the host / protocol variant rules (P3-01) that share `hopProblem`.
