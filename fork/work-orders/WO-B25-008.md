## Class

`checks/bot-parity.ts` counts every refused bot fetch toward the bots it names and toward severity. A bot that the site refuses only after a lockout or an IP check (some fetches get the full page, later ones a lockout page) is named as "refused", and a refused search crawler makes the finding S1, though the handbook says a UA-spoofed fetch from our IP cannot reproduce how the site treats the real crawler.

## Rule (default)

- Per bot, over its compared fetches in the run (robots-allowed, browser got 200, 429 excluded — as today):
  - **refused (reproduced)**: every compared fetch of that bot failed (≥ 2 fetches). Bodies may differ per request (aweandorderorganizing.com's 7 Cloudflare 403s are 4 545 B each with 7 different `sha256` values), so body identity is not required.
  - **not tested from our IP**: the bot got the browser's page on ≥ 1 fetch and a refusal on ≥ 1 other fetch in the same run (lockout, rate or IP check keyed on our requests, not on the UA). Listed in the detail as "<bot>: not tested from our IP (n of m fetches refused after k full pages; a UA-spoofed fetch cannot reproduce how the site treats the real crawler, handbook §0 Mode 1)".
- Only refused bots go into the title line, `search crawlers:` / `AI crawlers:` and severity (ADR-037 unchanged). No refused bot left → no finding; P9-01 keeps its status and the note lists the not-tested bots.
