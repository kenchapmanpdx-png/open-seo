# WO-B22-002: grade-spread report: which items fail on nearly every site

- Batch: B22 · Class: HEAVY · Size: S
- Contracts: none (read-only analysis)
- Depends: —

## Problem

- Of the eight goldens (B20 run 97) five grade F and three D; the field-test sites also land D / F. A scale where every site fails separates nothing for a client. Cause (items too strict, genuinely universal defects, or the cap) is SPECULATIVE until measured.

## Spec

1. `fork/scripts/grade-spread.ts <runs-dir...>`: reads the latest stored `report.json` per site under `fork/runs/` (gitignored, Ken's computer) for the 8 goldens + every field-test site; skips runs with check errors (`golden-score` rule), non-final runs are kept but marked.
2. Writes `fork/reports/grade-spread-<date>.md`: (a) grade and uncapped / final histogram; (b) per phase mean score; (c) per item: sites OPEN / PARTIAL / PASS / N/A counts, ordered by OPEN rate; items OPEN on ≥ 80 % of sites (with ≥ 8 sites) listed with their severity default, effect (from WO-B22-001 when present) and the points they cost on average; (d) how many sites are capped and by which findings.
3. Proposals are rows for Ken (never code): "item X fails on 11 of 12 sites; handbook says S2 default; keep, or re-read the check". Default: no change. A suspected check defect (an item failing on ≥ 80 % of sites with a verdict in a field-test ledger) becomes a queue row with a red case, per the prevention rule.

## Guard

- `grade-spread.test.ts` (`fork/scripts/lib`): three fixture reports → exact histogram, OPEN rates and the ≥ 80 % list; a run with a check error is skipped; fewer than 8 sites → the ≥ 80 % list is omitted with the reason.

## Do not

- Change scoring, severities or the registry; read ranking, traffic or backlink data.
