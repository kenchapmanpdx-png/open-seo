## Spec

- `renderClientReportHtml(report, registry, { screenshots?: Map<url, dataUri> })` → string. Sections per ADR-012 §3: grade card (grade, final, handbook version, date, cap reason in plain words) · what we checked · top fixes (plain title, why it matters, effort, ≤ 5 URLs) · findings by severity with evidence excerpts (escaped) · passes (count + ≤ 15 lines) · needs owner access · platform limits · method note (no ranking/traffic data; LLM-judged items marked 'AI-reviewed').

- Budget: if > 480 KB with screenshots → drop screenshots to the top-fix ones; still > 480 KB → no screenshots; then truncate evidence excerpts to 300 chars. Never exceed 500 KB.
