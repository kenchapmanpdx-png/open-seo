## Spec

- Pure functions over `prose: {h2, text}[]`; no I/O, no clock.
- Tokens per SCALED-COPY §4.2: word = letters + inner `'` / `-` (numbers are not words for E4, E5); sentence split on `.?!` + whitespace + uppercase / digit / quote, abbreviations `e.g.`, `i.e.`, `vs.`, `Mr.`, `Mrs.`, `Ms.`, `Dr.`, `Inc.`, `Ltd.`, `Co.`, `U.S.`, `No.` do not split; proper noun = capitalized word not at sentence start, not `I`.
- One function per metric id in §4.3 order, returning `CopyMetric` (`value`, `threshold` string as in §4.3 or `null` for E5 / E10 / E15, `over`). E13: SD of sentence lengths in 300-word windows, step 50; needs ≥ 300 prose words else `value: null, over: false`. E6b: H2 sections with ≥ 50 prose words; no H2 → `null`. E7b: flag when any one phrase rate > 1 per 500 words; `value` = the highest rate.
- Word lists and thresholds copied verbatim from §4.3 into constants (one place).
- Export `measureProse(prose): CopyMetric[]` (all 17, report order) and `proseWords(prose)`.
