## Spec

- `cron-tick.sh`: POST `http://127.0.0.1:3001/cdn-cgi/handler/scheduled?cron=*/5+*+*+*+*`; at 03:17 local also the daily expression. First verify the endpoint answers under `run-local.sh prod` (`vite preview`); if it does not → ESCALATE (fallback route needs a HEAVY decision) and ship the rest.
- `backup.sh`: `pg_dump --format=custom "$SUPABASE_DB_URL"` (sourced like run-local.sh, never printed) → `~/openseo-backups/YYYY-MM-DD.dump`; prune to 14 daily + 8 weekly (Sundays).
- Units: user units, `Restart=always` (app), timers `Persistent=true`; `install-units.sh` copies to `~/.config/systemd/user/`, `daemon-reload`, `enable --now`. Requires WSL `systemd=true` — check `systemctl --user is-system-running`; unavailable → BLOCKED-KEN (one line: enable systemd in /etc/wsl.conf).
